# Downtime Activities

Downtime is the time between expeditions, measured in whole days. During downtime a character may carry out one activity at a time and cannot start a new one until the current one is finished. Crafting an item takes one day for every 50 gold pieces of its value, and the character must pay half the value in materials up front. Training with a new skill takes 30 days and costs 25 gold pieces per day for a teacher. Carousing for a week gives a chance of meeting a useful contact but costs 10 gold pieces per day. Recovering from a long illness requires 10 days of rest. A character who is interrupted during an activity loses half of the days already spent. The game master decides whether an activity is possible in the current town and may add a cost or a delay to reflect local conditions.
