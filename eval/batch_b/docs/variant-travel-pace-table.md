# Travel Pace Table (reworded)

Travel pace sets how far a party moves in a day and how alert it stays. At a slow pace the party moves 18 miles in a day and can move stealthily. At a normal pace it covers 24 miles in a day. At a fast pace it covers 30 miles in a day but suffers a penalty to noticing hidden dangers. Rough terrain halves the distance covered. A party that travels for more than eight hours in a day must make an endurance check for each member, and a failure adds one level of fatigue. Mounted travel adds 50 percent to the distance at a normal pace. Boats on a river travel 36 miles a day with the current and 12 miles a day against it.

| Pace | Miles per day | Stealth | Notice dangers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slow | 18 | yes | normal |
| Normal | 24 | no | normal |
| Fast | 30 | no | penalty |
