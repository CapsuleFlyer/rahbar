# Sage Advice Compendium

Rulings in this edition replace the earlier answers where they differ.

Can a character take two bonus actions in one turn? No. A character can take only one bonus action on a turn, even if two features offer one, and must choose which to use.

Does the Help action require the helper to stay beside the target? No. The helper may take the action and then move away, because the benefit comes from the action itself and not from staying close.

If a hidden creature attacks, does it stay hidden? A creature that attacks from hiding is revealed after the attack resolves, whether or not the attack hits, unless a special ability says otherwise.

Can a character move through the space of an enemy? Not unless the enemy is two sizes larger or two sizes smaller. Moving through a friendly space is allowed, but the character cannot end the move there.

Does a reaction refresh before or after the start of a character's turn? It refreshes at the start of the character's own turn, before any other effect that begins on that turn.
