Heavy rain slows overland travel by one step on the pace table and makes tracking impossible. Fog limits sight to 30 feet and gives every creature the benefit of concealment against attacks from further away. A blizzard doubles the cost of every mile and forces a check each hour to avoid losing the way. Extreme heat requires a double ration of water for each day, and extreme cold requires fur or a fire at every rest. Strong wind makes ranged attacks harder and puts out open flames.
