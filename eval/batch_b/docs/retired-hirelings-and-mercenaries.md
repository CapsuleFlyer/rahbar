# Hirelings and Mercenaries

Hirelings are non-adventuring helpers paid a daily wage: porters, guides, torchbearers and guards. A party that wants help on a long expedition negotiates a wage with each hireling before leaving town. Unskilled hirelings cost 2 silver pieces per day. Skilled hirelings, such as scouts and healers, cost 2 gold pieces per day. A hireling does not fight unless the contract says so, and a combat contract doubles the wage. Hirelings who are badly treated may desert at the next safe settlement, taking any equipment they carry. The party is responsible for feeding its hirelings, and a hireling who goes hungry for three days refuses to travel further. Loyalty is tracked on a scale from 1 to 10 and starts at 6. Paying a bonus, sharing treasure or saving a hireling from danger raises it by 1. Abandoning a hireling lowers it by 3.
